# What is Govator
Govator is a learning platform for schools: a website and app (an online platform or site) where students learn coding, electronics and robotics. Students learn through hands-on courses. Instead of typing code, they snap together colourful blocks, and then run their programs on a real kit or in the built-in Simulator.

# Who made Govator
Govator is made by Grok Learning, an ed-tech company that builds learning tools for schools.

# What you can do on this platform
You can follow courses such as IoT Foundations and Robotics Foundation, open lessons with step-by-step instructions, build programs in the Block Workspace, test them in the Simulator, connect a real IoT or Robotics kit with Connect Kit, and track your progress. Every lesson ends with a short quiz, and each course finishes with a project.

# Who uses it
Students use Govator to learn and build projects. Teachers see their batches in the Class Dashboard and build reports. School admins manage teachers, students and lab kits for their school. Organisation admins look after many schools.

# What the Govator Assistant can help with
The assistant answers questions about the courses and lessons, every block and what it does, the sample block programs, the platform tools, the IoT and Robotics kits, and how to use the platform. It cannot help with topics outside Govator.
