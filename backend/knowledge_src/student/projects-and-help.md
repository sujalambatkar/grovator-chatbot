# My Projects
Every program you build is saved in My Projects. Open it to continue, rename it or delete it. Deleted projects cannot be brought back, so ask your teacher before deleting.

# Your progress
The progress bar on each subject shows how many lessons you have finished. You can see your own progress only. Your marks and reports are shared by your teacher.

# Getting help
If you are stuck, read the hint under the lesson step first. Then ask your teacher in class. The assistant can explain how to use the platform, the kits and block coding, but it cannot do your homework answers or share other students' information.

# Staying safe
Never share your password. Do not type your phone number, address or other personal details into the chat.
