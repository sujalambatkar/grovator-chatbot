# What it is
Connect Kit links your computer to the IoT or Robotics kit over USB.

# Connecting
Plug in the USB cable, open the subject and press Connect Kit, then choose your kit from the list. The status light shows the state: green means connected, blinking blue means connecting and red means a problem.

# Troubleshooting
If the light is red, unplug the USB cable, wait 5 seconds, plug it in again and retry. Try a different USB port if it still fails. If the kit says its software is old, press Update Firmware and keep the cable plugged in until the green light returns. Never unplug during an update.
